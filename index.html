GOOD TO KNOW

What are Candle Pearls?

Loose wax pearls designed to be poured into a suitable candle-safe vessel and used with a wick.

Can I reuse them?

The ritual is designed around replacing the used wick and reusing the remaining clean pearls.

What kind of vessel should I use?

Use only stable, heat-resistant, non-flammable vessels suitable for candle use and follow the final safety instructions supplied with Zakia.
