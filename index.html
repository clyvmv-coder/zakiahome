Home is not a place.

Zakia Home is inspired by the moments that bring us back to ourselves: the glow of a fire, the silence of a forest, the horizon over water, the warmth of wood and the comfort of a familiar room.

Our objects are made to create atmosphere without noise — simple, tactile and timeless.
